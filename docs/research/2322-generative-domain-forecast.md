already admitted for the CAR/CDR family:

- roots 101 and 110;

Reproduce:

    python3 scripts/research-2322-generative-domain-forecast.py \
      --min-width 3 --max-width 8 \
      --out /tmp/generative-domain-forecast

Expected invariant:

    selector_owned(Dn) = 2^(n-2)
    capacity(Dn)       = 2^n
    selector_share     = 1/4

So the selector family doubles at every added bit while preserving a constant
one-quarter share of the exact-width coordinate space.

The significance is not that one quarter of every future domain must be
allocated. The significance is that those selector coordinates do not require
new independent semantic invention once the root+law model is admitted.

## Accounting boundary

The script reports unexplained_coordinates, not “new functions”.

Unexplained coordinates may later be:
- genuine independent residue;
- generated by another independently proved law;
- intentionally unallocated;
- invalid under a future stronger grammar.

The script therefore cannot be used as an allocation authority.
