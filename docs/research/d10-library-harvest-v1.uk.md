## Відібрані сім'ї

**Translation protocol:** finite vocabularies, envelope/payload/ambiguity/review validation та admission-decision mapping.

**Immutable world ancestry:** depth walking, journal prefix, ancestor tests і common-ancestor operations.

**UTF-8 validation:** byte/range/continuation та second-byte boundary laws.

**Lisp-FS:** package/event decisions, address-presence invariants і deterministic recovery.

**Dimension algebra:** validation, add, merge and negate dimension exponents.

**Prolog→Datalog:** explicit structural projection from observed substitutions into Datalog fact data.

**Structured narration:** controlled presentation of proved and malformed reasoning outcomes.
